Morning — E1 shared-code seam: removed the Darlington_in_FTC copy Android submodule entirely and ported its real teleop/auto/subsystem logic into the main project, reorganized into three sections — Main (robot-code), Config (shared/RobotHardwareConfig), Setup (sim/RobotSimHarness).

Afternoon — E2/E5 pivot, real physics: merged vr_physics-master fully into sim/vendor (no more sibling-directory ../ references). Discovered vr_physics-master already has a mature, working ODE4J physics engine — it was just wired to its own demo bot (UltimateBot, a 2020 "Ultimate Goal" robot), never to this project's code. Rather than building a second ODE4J world from scratch against the bare SimMotor stub (the original E2 plan), built a bridge instead: VrPhysicsMotor/Servo/IMU/HardwareMap (in org.darbots.sim.vrbridge) implement IMotor/IServo/IIMU/IHardwareMap over vr_physics-master's real physics-backed classes, a new DarbotsBot chassis (box + 4 mecanum wheels, real ODE4J force/torque kinematics, modeled on UltimateBot's pattern) replaces the demo bot as the app's default, and two thin OpMode classes (DarbotsTeleop, DarbotsAuto) call the same already-ported TeleopDrive/MecanumDrive/Shooters/AutoRoute — no duplicated logic. App launches and discovers the new classes via reflection with no exceptions; not yet visually confirmed driving correctly — verify by running ./gradlew :sim:run yourself. Chassis mass/dimensions are placeholder estimates, not yet tuned to the real robot.

Risks to watch
ODE4J mecanum stability risk is now much lower than originally feared — DarbotsBot reuses UltimateBot's already-proven force/torque kinematics rather than untested from-scratch physics code. The DRIVE_MODE=KINEMATIC fallback flag is deprioritized accordingly.
Chassis mass/dimensions in DarbotsBot are placeholder estimates for a generic 18"×18" robot, not the real robot's numbers — drive behavior will be off until tuned.
Real-hardware deployment was deliberately dropped from this pass (sim-only for now) — when it's picked back up, it needs four adapter classes (RealMotor/RealServo/RealIMU/RealHardwareMap implementing the shared interfaces) plus a getVoltage() addition to IHardwareMap for feedforward voltage compensation, which the sim port currently fakes with a fixed nominal voltage.
